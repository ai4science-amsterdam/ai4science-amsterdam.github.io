Come join us for a one-day working session where students and postdocs (ideally 10 to 15) each bring one research problem and use [Lemma][3], a web platform for planning, running and writing up research with an AI agent, to take it from a question to a written-up result. A scientist from [Axiomatic AI][4] is in the room or on call for questions the room cannot answer. You bring the problems. We bring the tool, the structure, and access for the day.

This hackathon is primarily intended for PhD students and postdocs in the physical sciences, mathematics and engineering. The day works best for people who already know a problem well enough to judge whether an answer is right. Below is a tentative schedule for the day.
